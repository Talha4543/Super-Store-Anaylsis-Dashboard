📊 Super Store Sales Analysis Dashboard
---------------------------------------

This project presents an interactive **Super Store Sales Dashboard** designed to analyze key business metrics such as revenue, profit, sales distribution, and customer segments. The dashboard provides valuable insights to support data-driven decision-making.

🚀 Key Highlights
-----------------

*   💰 **Total Revenue:** $2.6M
    
*   📦 **Total Quantity Sold:** 38K
    
*   📈 **Total Profit:** $286.4K
    
*   📊 **Profit Margin:** 11.09%
    

📌 Dashboard Features
---------------------

### 🌍 Sales by State

*   Visualizes regional performance across the United States
    
*   Highlights high-performing and low-performing states
    
*   Categorized by regions (Central, East, South, West)
    

### 🏙️ Sales by City

*   Displays top cities contributing to revenue
    
*   Helps identify major sales hubs and growth opportunities
    

### 📦 Sales by Sub-Category

*   Breaks down sales into product sub-categories
    
*   Top contributors include:
    
    *   Phones
        
    *   Machines
        
    *   Accessories
        
    *   Copiers
        

### 🚚 Sales by Ship Mode

*   Shows distribution of sales based on shipping methods:
    
    *   Standard Class (highest)
        
    *   Second Class
        
    *   First Class
        
    *   Same Day
        

### 👥 Sales by Segment

*   Analyzes customer segments:
    
    *   Consumer (largest share)
        
    *   Corporate
        
    *   Home Office
        

🎯 Purpose of the Project
-------------------------

The goal of this dashboard is to:

*   Identify sales trends and patterns
    
*   Analyze profitability across regions and categories
    
*   Understand customer behavior
    
*   Support strategic business decisions
